# Wireflow — what to sketch

Five frames. Draw interface **states** as small captioned variations, not as separate full pages. Draw the phone at about 6 × 12 cm and leave room beside it for the body and the object.

---

## Frame 1 — Collection page

- **Visitor's goal:** understand what to do, then pick an object.
- **Visible UI:** title and tagline, "About 20 minutes", the three steps (Find a detail / Explore a gesture / Meet its story), the "Touch is optional" note, the **Explore the objects** button, the prototype notice, the "Where to look" panel ("Locations to be verified" box plus a numbered stop list), the **Explore by movement** dropdown, and the first two cards (image, specimen number, title, object type, clue, movement, location line, "Explore this gesture →").
- **Action:** scroll, change the filter, tap a card.
- **What changes:**
  - *Caption A:* the filter is set to "Pause", two cards remain, and the text reads "Showing 2 of 5 objects."
  - *Caption B (future):* the placeholder box becomes a map with numbered markers, and one popup is open with a link.

## Frame 2 — Object page, before reveal

- **Visitor's goal:** find the specific detail in real space.
- **Visible UI:** "← Back to all objects", "Example encounter — object and location unverified", the specimen tag, title, object type, and location line. Then **1 Find the detail**: a framed close-up with the "Illustration — replace with site photograph" label, the find prompt, and "Need a wider view?" (closed). Then two equal boxes: **2 Borrow a gesture** and **Another way to explore**. Then "Take a moment with the object. Return when you're ready." Then **3 Meet its story** with the **Reveal** button.
- **Action:** look from the screen to the surroundings and back.
- **What changes:** *Caption:* "Need a wider view?" is opened, and the context image appears under it.

## Frame 3 — The physical encounter (off-screen)

Draw the person, the object, and the phone, which is lowered or in a pocket. Show the relationship between them, such as a hand hovering over or resting on the rail, or a finger tracing its curve in the air at a distance.

- **Visitor's goal:** notice something through their body or eyes.
- **Visible UI:** none needed. The screen is unchanged.
- **Action:** the gesture *or* the alternative. Sketch both side by side, with touch on one side and looking or air-tracing on the other.
- **What changes:** only the visitor's attention. **The website does not sense this.**

## Frame 4 — Same object page, after reveal

- **Visitor's goal:** compare what they noticed with what's recorded, and enjoy the story.
- **Visible UI:** the same page with the button now reading **Hide the observation & story**. Below it, in order:
  - **What we can see**, with a "Demonstration note" tag
  - **An imagined moment**, in a dashed box tagged "Fiction, not research"
  - **Next time you pass**
  - **Field notes**: observed on, material, photograph, and source, showing "Not yet recorded" where unknown
- **Action:** tap Reveal, then read.
- **What changes:** *Caption:* existing content expands in place. There is no new page, no pop-up, and no auto-scroll. A second tap hides it again.

## Frame 5 — Continue

- **Visitor's goal:** move on.
- **Visible UI:** **Continue exploring**, with the **Explore another object** button and "Suggested next encounter (collection order, not a route): 02 Before the doorway".
- **Action:** tap one of these, or use "Back to all objects".
- **What changes:** the visitor returns to Frame 1 or opens Frame 2 for another object.

---

## The three phases in one encounter: 01 "A place for a hand"

| Phase | Body | Screen |
|---|---|---|
| **Anacrusis** (preparation) | Visitor walks toward the stairs and scans the rail. | The close-up shows a section with a different sheen. The prompt reads "Look for the place where the surface changes." |
| **Crusis** (the encounter) | Visitor rests a palm lightly on the rail and feels how it shapes the hand, *or* traces its curve in the air. | Unchanged. The phone is lowered. |
| **Metacrusis** (aftermath) | Visitor pauses and notices what they felt or saw. | They tap **Reveal**. The observation, imagined moment, and future invitation expand below. |

**Screen feedback is separate from the body:** tapping Reveal only expands content that is already there. Nothing on the site detects touch, location, or movement.
